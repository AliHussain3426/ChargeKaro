## Progress update (2026-11-15, P0.3 refund system implementation)
- **Domain/provider:** added `Refund` model (`server/src/modules/payments/refund.model.ts`) with payment/booking/customer/supplier refs, amount/currency, FULL/PARTIAL type, reason, status (`REQUESTED/PROCESSING/SUCCEEDED/FAILED/REJECTED`), provider/provider refund ref, idempotency key, requestedBy/role, processed/failure timestamps, and indexes (including partial unique for `paymentId+idempotencyKey` and `provider+providerRefundReference`). Extended provider contract (`providers/types.ts`) with `refund` capability and implemented sandbox simulated provider-confirmed refunds in `providers/sandbox.ts` (honest unsupported/failure simulation via reason marker).
- **Business logic/API:** added `refund.service.ts` and `refund.controller.ts`, wired routes in `payments/routes.ts`:
  - `POST /api/payments/:paymentId/refunds` (CUSTOMER own payment, ADMIN)
  - `GET /api/payments/:paymentId/refunds` (CUSTOMER owner, ADMIN)
  - `GET /api/payments/refunds` (CUSTOMER own, ADMIN)
  - `GET /api/payments/refunds/:refundId` (CUSTOMER owner, ADMIN)
  Enforces ownership/RBAC, eligibility (paid/partially refunded), amount validation against remaining refundable balance, per-payment lock to prevent concurrent over-refunds, in-flight duplicate prevention, idempotency-key replay, provider failure handling, retry-safe succeeded-total recomputation, and consistent updates to payment (`refundAmount`, `REFUNDED`/`PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`) and booking `paymentStatus`. Does not mark refund successful without provider-confirmed result; unsupported providers recorded as honest failed/unsupported outcomes.
- **Security/consistency:** prevents unauthorized/non-owner access (403/404 as applicable), duplicate/idempotent replay, concurrent over-refund, and state inconsistencies; provider failure leaves payment unrefunded while recording failure.
- **Frontend:** not modified (no refund UI added this unit; backend APIs ready; existing client flows preserved).

## Progress update (2026-11-15, P0.4 settlement/payout implementation)
- **Domain/models:** added `Settlement` (`server/src/modules/payments/settlement.model.ts`, per eligible provider-confirmed payment, unique `paymentId`) and `Payout` (`server/src/modules/payments/payout.model.ts`) with supplier/booking/payment refs, gross, configurable `platformFeeRate` snapshot, platform fee, refund deductions, other deductions, net payable, currency, settlement period, status, payout ref, timestamps, and auditable adjustment/status history. Commission rate configurable via `PLATFORM_COMMISSION_RATE` (default `0.1`, SRS policy pending); fee calculated on retained amount (`gross - succeeded refunds`) to avoid negative balances.
- **Calculation/reconciliation:** `settlement.service.ts` computes `Net Payable = Gross − Platform Fee − Refund Deductions − Other Approved Deductions` (rounded, non-negative), excludes pending/failed payments, uses succeeded refund totals only, is idempotent (per-payment and supplier locks, unique settlement per payment), and records auditable adjustments when refunds/recalculation occur after accrual/approval/processing.
- **Lifecycle/APIs:** settlement states `PENDING/CALCULATED/APPROVED/PROCESSING/PAID/FAILED/CANCELLED`; payout states `PENDING/APPROVED/PROCESSING/PAID/FAILED/CANCELLED` with validated transitions. Admin approval and internal/manual payout-record workflow implemented; **`PAID` is explicitly rejected without verified external payout/provider confirmation** and no bank transfer is simulated/claimed. Supplier APIs: `/api/payments/settlements/me`, `/api/payments/settlements/balance/me`, `/api/payments/payouts/me`. Admin APIs: `/api/admin/settlements`, `/api/admin/settlements/:settlementId`, `/api/admin/settlements/suppliers/:supplierId/reconcile`, `/api/admin/settlements/:settlementId/approve`, `/api/admin/payouts`, `/api/admin/payouts/suppliers/:supplierId`, `/api/admin/payouts/:payoutId/status`.
- **Integration/security:** best-effort reconciliation hooked on confirmed payment and succeeded refund; RBAC/supplier isolation enforced; no internal provider secrets exposed.
